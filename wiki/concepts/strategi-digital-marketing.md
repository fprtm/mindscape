# Strategi Digital Marketing (Harvard)

## Summary

Digital marketing bukan cuma konten/iklan/sosmed — tapi cara berpikir berbasis data: tentukan objektif, pahami customer, ukur dampak nyata (materi Harvard Business School via Ferry Irwandi, Malaka).

## Details

Dari `[[wiki/sources/2026-08-28-materi-strategi-digital-marketing-harvard]]` (126 lines):

- Objektif → customer insight → eksekusi → measurement.
- Lawan miskonsepsi `digital marketing = konten doang`.
