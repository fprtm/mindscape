# Marketing Funnel

## Summary

Banyak pebisnis ngelewatin step funnel tanpa sadar. Fix practical: improve customer service + pakai Whatsapp Business (@WhatsApp, collab Meta) untuk nurture biar sales maksimal.

## Details

Dari `[[wiki/sources/2026-08-28-trik-marketing-yang-semua-orang-wajib-tau]]` (Raymond Chin, 256 lines):

- Funnel ke-skip → sales nggak maksimal.
- Solusi: customer service + Whatsapp Business.
